# Monarch Migration

An animated map story of the yearly monarch butterfly migration between the Mexican overwintering forests and North America, told across one year.

## Language

**Chapter**:
A titled passage of the story that is active from its start day of the year until the next one begins.
_Avoid_: Step, section, slide

**Generation**:
One of the four successive butterfly cohorts that make up a migration year, numbered by birth order after the Super generation.
_Avoid_: Brood, wave

**Super generation**:
The long-lived generation born in late summer that flies south, overwinters in Mexico and starts the spring flight north. It is not "Generation 1".
_Avoid_: First generation, Methuselah generation

**Dominant generation**:
The Generation with the most butterflies on the move on a given day. On days with no butterflies on the move there is none.
_Avoid_: Current generation, active generation
